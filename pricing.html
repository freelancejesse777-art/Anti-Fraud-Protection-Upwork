Questions

Can I cancel any time?

Yes — from the dashboard, no email required, takes effect immediately.

What happens to my scan history if I downgrade?

It's trimmed back to the last 24 hours; nothing older is deleted immediately, but Free tier only displays the most recent day.

Do extra Team seats cost more?

Team includes 5 seats. Additional seats are $6/mo each — reach out via support once you're on the Team plan to add them.
